Country Availability

HashLoop may make simulator features broadly available, while monetary features are only enabled where provider, payout, safety, and operational checks allow.

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Current public status

Monetary country availability is not publicly enabled yet. HashLoop is still completing provider and payout onboarding. A country being visible in an app store, or a user being able to install the app, does not mean rewards or withdrawals are available in that country.

Safe launch policy

FeatureRecommended availability
Mining simulation and educational screensAvailable where the app is legally distributed and technically supported.
Provider-sponsored rewardsOnly in countries explicitly accepted by the ad/reward provider and enabled by the server.
Lightning withdrawalOnly in countries accepted by the payout provider and after internal risk checks.

How country controls should work

Country decisions must be made by the backend using a trusted server-side country signal, not GPS or a client-controlled APK value. The Owner Dashboard should show each country as Allowed, Reward Stop, Withdrawal Stop, or Blocked, with a reason and an audit record.

Recommended first monetary launch: enable no country until the selected ad provider and payout provider explicitly confirm the model and supported countries. Then enable a small allowlist—such as Indonesia only if both providers approve it—and expand gradually.
