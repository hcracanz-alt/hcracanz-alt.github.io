Support

Help, safety information, and release status for HashLoop.

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Support channel

Contact us at support.hashloopofficial@gmail.com. Do not send account passwords, OTP codes, recovery codes, or Lightning invoices to anyone claiming to be HashLoop support.

For current public project status, see the HashLoop GitHub profile.

Before public release

Contact support.hashloopofficial@gmail.com for account access, device recovery, withdrawal status, and reports of suspected fraud. Some features remain in development.

Security reminder

HashLoop staff should never ask for your password, OTP, device code, wallet seed phrase, or private key. HashLoop does not request deposits to unlock rewards or withdrawals.
