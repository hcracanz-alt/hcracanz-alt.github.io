Mining Simulation

HashLoop is a mobile-first mining simulation. It does not mine Bitcoin or any cryptocurrency on a user’s phone.

Effective date: 24 September 2026
Product disclosure

What HashLoop is

HashLoop uses virtual mining power (“GH”) as a simulation unit for in-app reward rules. It does not use a device’s CPU, GPU, battery, or hardware to perform real blockchain mining.

HashLoop is not a deposit product, investment, staking product, miner sale, cloud-mining contract, or profit-sharing membership.

How activities work

Eligible activities may activate time-limited simulated power or bonuses under the rules published in the app. Sponsor activities only qualify when a provider callback is verified by HashLoop’s server. A video view alone is not treated as verified until that server verification occurs.

The server—not the APK—determines account state, eligibility, reward contracts, and balances.

Rewards and withdrawal

Any monetary reward, where enabled, is subject to a published reward rule, reserve capacity, account/security checks, country availability, provider availability, and withdrawal rules. There is no guaranteed earning, return, or payout.

Do not pay anyone to obtain HashLoop power, levels, access, or withdrawal priority. HashLoop does not sell mining equipment or investment plans.
