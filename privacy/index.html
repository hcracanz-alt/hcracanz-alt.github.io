Privacy Policy

How HashLoop and this informational website handle data.

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Scope

This policy covers the public HashLoop/racanz.studio website and describes the intended data handling for the HashLoop app while it is in development. It will be updated before a public monetized release if integrations or applicable requirements change.

Data used by the app

When an account is created or a feature is used, HashLoop may process email address and verification status, account/device-binding information, security and anti-abuse signals, activity/reward records, referral information, withdrawal requests and Lightning invoice data, and support communications.

When an approved advertising or offer provider is active, that provider may process its own advertising/device identifiers and event information under its own policies. HashLoop does not send provider secrets to the app.

Why data is used

Data is used to operate the service, authenticate accounts, recover devices, verify provider events, prevent fraud, maintain financial/audit records, process eligible withdrawals, comply with legal obligations, and support users.

Website hosting

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary technical request data under its own privacy policy. This website does not intentionally collect passwords, payment cards, or seed phrases.

Retention and rights

Financial, ledger, security, and audit history may be retained when needed to protect the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations, including after an account is disabled. You may request information or correction by emailing support.hashloopofficial@gmail.com.
