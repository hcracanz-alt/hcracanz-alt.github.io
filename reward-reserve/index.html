Reward Reserve

The HashLoop Reward Reserve is the controlled operational budget used for eligible rewards. It is not a user deposit or investment fund.

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Purpose

The Reserve is used to limit new monetary reward obligations. Before a new monetary reward contract is activated, HashLoop’s backend must confirm sufficient reserve capacity for its maximum liability.

Important limits

  • The Reserve is not customer money, an investment pool, a custody account, or a claim on crypto assets.
  • Users do not own a share of the Reserve and do not receive profit sharing.
  • Rate changes apply prospectively to newly activated contracts. Active eligible contracts retain their server-recorded rate snapshot.
  • If capacity is insufficient, the system should stop creating new monetary liability rather than create an unfunded obligation.

Emergency controls

Emergency controls may stop new reward activations, sponsors, withdrawals, registration, or providers when needed for safety. They must not silently erase legitimate earned balances or legitimate active reward contracts.
