HashLoop is a mobile-first mining simulation with server-controlled account and reward flows. It is not real Bitcoin mining, a deposit product, an investment, or a promise of earnings.

How HashLoop works Reward Reserve

Clear product boundaries

MiningSimulation only. The app does not use your phone to mine cryptocurrency.
MoneyNo deposits, investments, staking, miner purchases, pay-to-earn memberships, or profit sharing.
Sponsor activityOnly server-verified provider events may be eligible for sponsor rewards.
RewardsAny monetary reward is subject to reserve capacity, country/provider availability, security checks, and published rules.

Public availability

HashLoop is in development. Monetary country availability and advertising-provider onboarding are not publicly enabled until the relevant providers approve the model and operating countries.
