FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers
before you begin.

Does HashLoop actually mine Bitcoin?+

No. HashLoop is a mining simulation. It does not mine Bitcoin or run mining on your device.

Do I need to deposit or buy a miner?+

No. HashLoop does not offer deposits, investments, staking, miner purchases, or pay-to-earn memberships.

How do sponsor activities work?+

Sponsor activities can only count after verification by an approved provider and the server. Provider availability varies by release and country.

Are all features available in every country?+

Not always. Availability depends on your country, providers, development status, and published rules.
